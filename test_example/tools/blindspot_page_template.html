What the map says

How it was measured

  • Spots are spread evenly over the outer housing skin of the reference solution, mm apart. Outer means skin someone can see: from just off the surface, rays cast over the half sphere it faces reach open space. From the inner skin of the hollow housing almost none do. Spots inside the widening strip and the margins around the controls are not edited, because the task asks for changes there.
  • At every spot four real edits are made, one at a time, on the reference's own SolidWorks capture: a 0.15 mm step at the surface tolerance, a 0.5 mm boss, a 3 mm boss and a through hole, each 8 mm across, the same discs as the coverage sweep shipped with the grader, with one fix described below.
  • Two more edits leave the shape as it is and must not be flagged: a split line (the disc becomes its own face at zero height, as an imprinted sketch makes it) and a different tessellation (the disc meshed finer, each vertex moved up to 0.05 mm, as a mesh at another quality setting sits off the true surface). Every flag there is a false alarm.
  • Each edited part is graded by the real check. An edit counts as caught when no unrequested changes drops below full marks. That is grades, all offline from the capture, no SolidWorks needed.
  • Colour between spots is interpolated from nearby spots that face the same way. Each spot is one deterministic trial, so read patches, not single points; Show spots marks where the measurements are.
  • The edits change the housing surface only, so this maps the skin part of no unrequested changes. The criterion's span and body-shape checks are not exercised here.

Use it to prove a change

The map is part of a small audit that every change to the grader has to pass. It grades the same spots before and after the change and fails if the reference loses marks, a broken example scores higher, a harmless edit is newly flagged, or anything caught before is missed. Pointing it at another grader or part means swapping two functions: the one that scores an edited part and the one that says where the task allows changes.

python3 tools/blindspot_audit.py run audit/before
python3 tools/blindspot_audit.py run audit/after --spots-from audit/before
python3 tools/blindspot_audit.py compare audit/before audit/after
